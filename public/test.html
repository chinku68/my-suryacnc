Our Story

Regenerative family farm in Barham, NSW, raising heritage Berkshire pigs and pastured chickens on open land. Their on-farm butchery produces 100% free-range, antibiotic-free, chemical-free meats alongside award-winning French-style charcuterie and smallgoods, all delivered fresh through their farm-to-door subscription model.

Our Commitment

Regenerative farming practices
Heritage breed preservation (Berkshire pigs)
On-farm butchery for maximum freshness
Respect for Barapa Barapa country
